About this platform

The library, extended past the shelves.

SAJS Digital Library is Seth Anandram Jaipuria School's catalogue, reading community, and discussion space in one place. Every book in the physical library gets a permanent catalogue ID — SAJS-001 and up — along with a full breakdown of its main idea, characters, setting, plot, and themes, so students can decide what to read next without guessing from a spine.

How accounts work

Every account starts as a student account. Teachers are given catalogue and moderation access by a school admin — there's no self-service way to become a teacher, which keeps the reviews and discussions accountable to real staff.

Catalogue IDs

The SAJS- prefix is permanent — it's the school's own call-number system, assigned automatically and in order as new books are catalogued by a teacher.
