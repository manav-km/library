Seth Anandram Jaipuria School

The library, extended past the physical shelf.

SAJS Digital Library is a centralized digital hub for Seth Anandram Jaipuria School's physical collection, literary community, and academic discussions. It empowers students, faculty, and administrators to discover, reflect on, and discuss literature together.

Platform Features & Pillars

🏷️

Permanent Catalogue IDs

Every book receives a permanent call number starting with SAJS-001. Along with traditional metadata, each entry details the book's main idea, key themes, character profiles, plot arc, setting, and moral lessons.

💬

Reviews & Live Discussions

Students can rate and review books they have finished, and engage in real-time book discussions with peers. Active teacher and admin moderation maintains a respectful environment.

📊

Personalized Reading Dashboard

Students, teachers, and admins have access to a personal dashboard tracking their recently viewed books, submitted reviews, favourite genres, and customizable profile settings.

🛡️

Accountable Role System

Users are assigned Student, Teacher, or Admin roles. Privileged management features (catalogue CRUD, review moderation, and role promotion) are organized under a secure **Manage** section.

Account Governance & Customization

Users have total control over their personal profile information. Students, teachers, and admins can edit their Name, Email, Class, Section, Roll Number, Subject, Favourite Genre, Bio, and Profile Picture at any time directly from their dashboard edit modal.

Security Note: The User ID (UID) is permanently assigned by Firebase Authentication to guarantee data integrity across reviews and activity logs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a new book to be added?

Speak with your English or Literature teacher. Faculty members can add new titles directly using the Catalogue management tools in the Manage portal.

How are reviews moderated?

All reviews are visible immediately. Teachers and admins review submissions in the Review Moderation tab and can remove inappropriate content if necessary.

Can I update my profile picture and reading bio?

Yes! Click on Dashboard, then select Edit profile. You can update your name, email, avatar, bio, academic details, and favourite genre.
