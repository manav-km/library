Library & Platform Guidelines

To ensure the SAJS Digital Library remains a safe, educational, and inspiring space for everyone, we ask all students, teachers, and admins to follow these core guidelines.

General Platform Rules

  • Strictly Academic: The SAJS Library site is a school platform. Any behavior that violates the school's general code of conduct is strictly prohibited.
  • No Spam or Advertising: Do not post commercial content, external website links, or repetitive messages in discussions.
  • Respect Intellectual Property: Do not upload PDFs or files that violate copyright laws. Ensure all uploaded material is either educational or publicly licensed.
  • Zero Tolerance for Bullying: Any form of harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks in chat or reviews will result in immediate account suspension and referral to school administration.
  • Bug & Issue Reporting: Report any bugs, display glitches, or system issues directly to the developer by clicking "Contact Support" in your dashboard to send an official email.

Guidelines for Students

Honest & Thoughtful Reviews

Only review books you have actually finished reading. Write thoughtful reflections in your reviews, covering what you liked, what you learned, and constructive ideas for improvement. Avoid low-effort or empty reviews.

Respectful Discussions

The community forums are for academic and literary discussions. Be polite, encourage other readers, and respect differing viewpoints. Chatting about non-book related topics or spamming the discussions is not permitted.

Keep Profile Details Accurate

Always keep your class, section, roll number, and class teacher name accurate. This helps teachers track reading progress and assign credit properly. Do not share your password or compromise your account security.

Inappropriate Content & Reporting

If you see inappropriate language, reviews, or chat posts, report them to a librarian or teacher immediately. Do not engage with or reply to disruptive posts.

Guidelines for Teachers & Admins

Catalogue Integrity

Ensure all newly added books have the correct Call ID (SAJS-###), complete metadata, proper genre classification, and accurate reading information (main idea, themes, characters, setting).

Responsible Operations

Please upload, edit, or delete books and media responsibly. Excessive rapid write/delete requests can cause the project database to hit usage signals or get flagged for high signals.

Responsible Moderation

Monitor the reviews and custom discussion threads. Remove reviews or messages that violate conduct rules, but encourage free expression of reading opinions and constructive criticism.

Announcements & Updates

Use the announcements tab to post weekly library events, reading recommendations, or scheduling updates. Keep announcements clear, relevant, and professional.
